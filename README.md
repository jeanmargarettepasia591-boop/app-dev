## Movie Overview
**Crazy Beautiful You** is a 2015 Filipino romantic comedy-drama film directed by *Mae Cruz-Alviar* and produced by ***Star Cinema***. Originally conceptualized as a typical teen romance, the story evolved into a deeply emotional family drama set against the scenic backdrop of Tarlac[^1].

* **Genre:** Romantic Comedy, Drama
* **Release Date:** February 25, 2015
* **Runtime:** `114 minutes`
* **Original Plan:** ~~Pure light-hearted rom-com~~ **Emotional family & romance drama**

### Plot Progression (Ordered List)
1. Jackie gets arrested for drag racing in Manila.
2. Her mother, Dr. Leah, brings her to a medical mission in Tarlac as a form of discipline.
3. Mayor Ito assigns his son, Kiko, to watch over Jackie and keep her out of trouble.
4. Jackie slowly opens up to the local Aeta community and begins falling for Kiko.
